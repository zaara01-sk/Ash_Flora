Flower Care Guide

how to keep your blooms fuzzy forever

💧

Keep Them Dry

Pipe cleaner flowers do not need water! Keep them away from moisture and spills to preserve shape.

🖌️

Gentle Dusting

Use a soft makeup brush or gentle hairdryer on cool setting to whisk away any dust.

☀️

Avoid Harsh Sunlight

Display indoors away from harsh direct midday sunlight to keep the vibrant chenille colours rich.
